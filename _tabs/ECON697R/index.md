## About

This Directed Study is designed to be an intensive reading in econometrics on a topic not covered in a regular course at Emory University. This course explores a variety of topics in counterfactual analysis with applications in causal inference literature. The course is based on student presentations and discussions among participants (students and faculty invited).

## Course Description

This reading study session explores the theoretical foundations, methodological advancements, and practical applications of counterfactual analysis in causal inference in depth. The course builds from the basic principles to advanced topics, incorporating sensitivity analyses, handling nonstationary data, and implementing counterfactual models.

## Content

### Topic 1: Introduction to Synthetic Control

- Motivation behind synthetic control
- Methodology behind this method
- Estimation and inference procedure

*Main reference: Abadie, Diamond, and Hainmueller (2010). Synthetic control methods for comparative case studies: Estimating the effect of California's tobacco control program. JASA.*

### Topic 2: Counterfactual Imputation with Predicted Errors

- Key challenges and issues with traditional SCM
- Addressing the issue of correlated errors in the SCM framework

*Main references: Medeiros (2024). Counterfactual Imputation: Comments on "Imputation of Counterfactual Outcomes when the Errors are Predictable." JBES; Gonçalves and Ng (2024).*

### Topic 3: Inference, Sensitivity Analysis, and Confidence Sets in SCM

- Generalizing the inference and testing procedure behind SCM
- Moving away from uniform treatment assignment probabilities
- Extension to cases of multiple treatments

*Main reference: Firpo and Possebom (2018). Synthetic control method: Inference, sensitivity analysis and confidence sets. Journal of Causal Inference.*

### Topic 4: Synthetic Control Methods from an Empirical Viewpoint

- SCM framework for answering causal questions
- Conditions under which SCM may be feasible and appropriate
- Limitations when requirements are not met

*Main reference: Abadie (2021). Using synthetic controls: Feasibility, data requirements, and methodological aspects. JEL.*

### Topic 5: Factor-Adjusted Regularized Method for Treatment Evaluation (FarmTreat)

- Issues of SCM in high-dimensional settings and correlated data
- The proposed methodology by Fan et al. (2022) to address these issues

*Main reference: Fan, Masini, and Medeiros (2022). Do we exploit all information for counterfactual analysis? Benefits of factor models and idiosyncratic correction. JASA.*

### Topic 6: Counterfactual Analysis and Inference with Nonstationary Data

*Main reference: Masini and Medeiros (2022). Counterfactual analysis and inference with nonstationary data. JBES.*
