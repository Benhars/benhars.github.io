Welcome to my professional page. I'm **Benjamin O. Harrison**, a third-year PhD student in Economics at [Emory University](https://economics.emory.edu/people/grad-students/harrison-benjamin.html/), with research interests in econometrics and causal inference.

Before coming to Emory, I earned a B.A. in Economics and Mathematics from the [University of Ghana, Legon](https://www.ug.edu.gh/), and an M.Sc. in Quantitative Economics through the [Erasmus Mundus Joint Degree Program](https://master-economics-qem.pantheonsorbonne.fr/), jointly awarded by [Ca'Foscari University of Venice](https://unive.it/) and the [Catholic University of Louvain](https://uclouvain.be/fr/index.html).

I enjoy thinking deeply about economic questions, learning new methods, and working on research that blends theory, data, and real-world policy relevance.

Outside of economics, I enjoy reading theology and biblical commentaries, hiking, and playing volleyball.
